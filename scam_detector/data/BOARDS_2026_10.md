# Job-board batch, October 2026 (collected Sept 30)

**What:** 25 real listings each from LinkedIn, Indeed, ZipRecruiter and Glassdoor, labeled by hand against
LABELING_RUBRIC.md.
- **Frozen:** `boards_2026_10.jsonl.sha256`.
- **Dropped:** one listing that couldn't be decided (a call center that requires buying your own headset:
  strictly "pay to work" under the rubric, but not fraud in intent). That leaves 99 rows: 77 legit,
  20 lead-gen, 2 scam.
- **Split:** 10 per site for rule fixing (`boards_2026_10_rules.jsonl`, 40 rows) and 15 per site for the
  model only (`boards_2026_10_train.jsonl`, 59 rows). The model trains on all 99. The frozen holdouts
  (`field_2026_09_holdout`, `field_2026_10_holdout`) remain the test.

**How it was read:**
- LinkedIn through its public guest job pages, text extracted from the HTML.
- Indeed, ZipRecruiter and Glassdoor through search results and a page reader. Their direct downloads
  are blocked, and that block was not bypassed. Listings that came back summarized instead of copied
  were skipped.

## Finding: live board listings are almost never scams
Searches aimed at where scams hide (remote data entry, personal assistant, reshipping, weekly pay,
mystery shopper) turned up:
- LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor: no scams. Likely ones had already been taken down (404) by the time
  they were read.
- ZipRecruiter: 2 scams, both the "$700 a week for 3–6 hours, work from home or on campus" student
  personal-assistant template, posted under "Jobsultant Solutions".

What these boards do carry is lead-gen: staffing wrappers that never name the client, aggregators,
travel "host agency" funnels paid in "income-earning potential", and commission-only recruiting funnels.

## Rule fixes (from the 40 rule-fixing rows only)
Lead-gen axis (scam_detector/leadgen.py and demo/engine.js):
- A staffing firm or reposter hiring for a client it never names: "recruiting on behalf of our
  client", "hiring for one of our clients", "About our client:" (6 points). It doesn't match a call
  center "answering calls on behalf of our business clients".
- Pay described as "income-earning potential" or "performance-based income" (4). It doesn't match
  "performance-based incentives" on a salaried job.
- "Build something of your own", "build an income from home", "be your own boss" (2).
- Funnel boilerplate: "begin a long-lasting career with limitless opportunity" (3).
- "our client" now also matches "our clients".

Fraud rules (rulepack core.json, version 2026.09.30.1; guard in rules.py and engine.js):
- `personal_channel` got a new `disclaimer` guard. An employer's own safety promise ("At no time will a
  conversation be moved to an alternative email") no longer counts as the scam it rules out. This fixed
  a false alarm on a real Indeed data-entry listing, which the model had also learned to distrust.
